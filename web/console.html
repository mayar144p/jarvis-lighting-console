For a light the GDTF Share does not have. One channel name per line, in DMX order — the names are what give the channels their controls.

A line may also give the channel's travel: Tilt = -117..117. That is what lets the desk offer degrees instead of a bare number, and it is worth doing for pan and tilt on any mover.
